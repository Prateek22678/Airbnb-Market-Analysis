# Assumptions Log

This document records all methodological decisions, data cleaning rules, statistical assumptions, and business logic applied throughout the Airbnb Market Analysis project for Austin, TX.

---

| ID | Date | Category | Decision / Assumption | Rationale | Impact / Limitations |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ASM-001 | 2026-10-14 | Setup | Target market: Austin, Texas (Inside Airbnb snapshot 2026-09-17) | High-volume market with vibrant tourism (SXSW, ACL, F1), predominantly English reviews (>99%), rich geospatial and seasonal data. | Analysis reflects post-summer 2026 market dynamics. |
| ASM-002 | 2026-10-14 | Architecture | SQLite relational model with Python processing pipeline | SQLite is lightweight, serverless, zero-configuration, fully ACID compliant, and easily queryable via standard SQL tools. | Query execution runs in-memory or on local disk; adequate for single-market scale. |
| ASM-003 | 2026-10-14 | Data Cleaning | Currency parsing: remove `$` and `,` and cast to `float64` | Raw prices stored as string formatted currency (e.g. `"$1,200.00"`). | 1,106 raw listings (9.95%) had missing price quotes and were excluded from pricing analysis. |
| ASM-004 | 2026-10-14 | Data Cleaning | Boolean conversion: `'t'` -> 1, `'f'` and nulls -> 0 | Normalizes text flags (`host_is_superhost`, `host_identity_verified`, `instant_bookable`) into binary integer indicators. | Nulls default conservatively to 0 (non-superhost / unverified). |
| ASM-005 | 2026-10-14 | Data Cleaning | Bathrooms & Capacity Imputation: Regex parsing + capacity group medians | Raw `bathrooms_text` contains strings like `"1.5 baths"`, `"Half-bath"`. Missing values in bedrooms/beds/bathrooms (<2.5%) are imputed using median values grouped by `accommodates`. | Preserves realistic architectural ratios between beds, bedrooms, and guest capacity. |
| ASM-006 | 2026-10-14 | Outlier Treatment | Price Outlier Filtering: Floor at $15/night, ceiling at 99.5th percentile ($3,080.62) | Minimum: Sub-$15 prices (10 listings) represent $0 placeholders or deposit glitches. Maximum: Prices above 99.5th percentile (50 listings) are mega-mansions that distort regression gradients and ANOVA variance assumptions. | Final cleaned sample retains 9,945 commercial listings (89.5% retention of raw; 99.4% of priced listings). |
| ASM-007 | 2026-10-14 | Feature Engineering | Forward Occupancy Proxy from Calendar: `count(available == 'f') / total_days` | Inside Airbnb marks booked or host-blocked days as `'f'`. While some blocked days are host personal use, aggregate blocking serves as industry standard occupancy proxy. | Occupancy proxy calculated over 30d (51.8%), 90d (40.6%), and 365d (37.6%) horizons. |
| ASM-008 | 2026-10-14 | Review Cleaning | Reviews text filtering: drop nulls and string length <= 3 characters | Excludes automated empty comments or one-letter noise (dropped 3,327 reviews). | Retains 650,134 valid guest reviews across 9,219 reviewed listings. |
| ASM-009 | 2026-10-15 | EDA Methodology | Minimum sample threshold of $n \ge 50$ for neighbourhood pricing rankings | Prevents low-volume peripheral ZIP codes with 5–10 listings from skewing citywide comparisons. | 25 major Austin municipal zones qualify for statistical ranking. |
| ASM-010 | 2026-10-15 | EDA Methodology | Capacity evaluation boundary: $1 \le \text{accommodates} \le 16$ | Standard Airbnb search filters cap guest capacity at 16; mega-group compounds above 16 represent specialized commercial venues. | Captures 99.8% of typical residential tourism listings. |
| ASM-011 | 2026-10-15 | EDA Methodology | Review timestamps as seasonal guest arrival proxies | Review submission dates correlate with checkout dates within 1–14 days. | Multi-year review trajectory directly reflects seasonal festival peaks (March SXSW and October ACL/F1). |
